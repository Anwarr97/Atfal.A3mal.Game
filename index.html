?Why Atfal A'mal

Kids see money being spent. Nobody shows them how it's earned.

Financial literacy

Income, spending, saving and sharing, learned through everyday situations instead of lectures.

Careers

Kids try the job themselves: treat patients, build a bridge, run a stand. Before they ever have to choose.

Community

Every mission helps the neighborhood, so kids see that work and money have an impact on people.

3Who it's for

One game, two levels for two age groups

6–8

Play and discover

  • Picture stories with voice-over instructions
  • Coins you can count, two choices at a time
  • Small, close goals: a biscuit, a ball, a kite
9–12

Plan and try your career

  • Full level budgets, comparing prices and pack sizes
  • The three career paths: clinic, build site, market stand
  • More than one right answer; kids explain their choice

Our promises to parents

No real money

All coins are pretend. No in-app purchases.

No ads aimed at kids

Nothing interrupts play or asks a child to buy.

Kids' data stays safe

No name, number or address needed to play.

Mistakes aren't punished

Every choice has a result, and kids can try another plan.

  • Partners appear as "Supported by" and co-host awareness campaigns
  • Partners get an impact report: kids reached and what they learned
